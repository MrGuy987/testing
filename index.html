Collection notice

This site can collect browser/platform information, screen dimensions, language, timezone, the current page and referrer, page activity, and network metadata (a one-way hash of the IP address and country code). The data is used for the connected-visitor dashboard and security. Telemetry starts only after you sign in and explicitly accept. Do not include personal or confidential information in page URLs or click labels.

Use the “Privacy / revoke consent” control to stop future telemetry from this browser. This demo does not provide email verification or a self-service deletion workflow; contact the site administrator for account/data deletion. The site operator should publish a retention period and privacy contact before real-world use.
